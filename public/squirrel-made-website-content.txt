## BRAND VOICE GUIDE

**Tone:** Warm, honest, and grounded. Not precious or pretentious — this is food for real kitchens made by real hands. Speak like a trusted farmer's market vendor who genuinely knows their craft.

**Keywords to lean into:** handcrafted, small-batch, real ingredients, nothing hidden, made with care, seasonal, honest food, your kitchen deserves better.

**Keywords to avoid:** gourmet (overused), artisan (overused), luxury, premium (implies exclusivity over quality).

## PAGE 2 — ABOUT US

**Page Heading:**
> Made by Hand. Made to Matter.

**Opening Paragraph:**
Squirrel Made Products was built around a frustration most food lovers know well: the gap between what food should taste like and what it usually does. The ingredients that fill most pantry shelves are chosen for shelf life and cost, not flavour or integrity.

We decided to close that gap — one small batch at a time.

**Section: What We Stand For**

We make olive oils, balsamic vinegars, and spice blends. That's it. We do those three things with as much care as we know how, using real, all-natural ingredients and small-batch production methods that keep quality where it belongs — at the centre.

Everything we make is crafted locally. Everything we use is honest. Nothing we sell requires a chemistry degree to understand.

**Section: Why Small Batch**

Small-batch production isn't a marketing phrase for us — it's a constraint we've chosen deliberately. Smaller runs mean fresher product. They mean we can adjust, taste, and improve without a warehouse full of inventory to write off. They mean seasonal ingredients can be seasonal, because we're not locked into a formula that has to be the same every month of the year.

The result is food that tastes alive — because it is.

**Section: Our Ingredients**

Our extra virgin olive oils start with single-origin Spanish EVOO, selected for its smooth, fruity character and clean flavour profile. Infusions are made with fresh herbs and aromatics — not flavour concentrates or extracts.

Our balsamic vinegars are aged traditionally. Our spice blends are built from real spices, freshly sourced, with no fillers and no anti-caking agents.

That's the whole story.

**Closing Line:**
> Good food doesn't need a long explanation. Neither do we.

## PAGE 4 — OUR PROMISE / CLEAN LABEL PAGE

**Page Heading:**
> What's in It. What Isn't.

**Intro:**
We think food transparency should be the baseline, not the exception. Here's exactly where we stand.

**Section: What Goes In**

Every Squirrel Made product is made from ingredients you'd find in a kitchen — not a lab. We use real herbs, real spices, real extra virgin olive oil, and real aged vinegar. When we say "basil infused," we mean basil. When we say "herb blend," we mean herbs.

**Section: What Stays Out**

We don't use:
- Artificial flavours or flavour concentrates
- Artificial colours
- Chemical preservatives
- Fillers or bulking agents
- Anti-caking agents in our spice blends
- Anything that doesn't belong in food

**Section: Why It Matters**

Artificial additives exist to make cheap ingredients taste like something, or to make food last longer than it naturally would. We'd rather make smaller batches with fresher ingredients than compromise on what goes in the jar.

When you buy Squirrel Made, you're buying food that respects your kitchen — and your judgment.

**Closing Statement:**
> If it's on our label, it's in the jar. If it's not on the label, it's not in the jar.
